How technicians get paid here

Hourly base plus commission on everything you sell and install. Read the structure, then run your own numbers below.

The short version

You earn an hourly base for every hour you work. On top of that you earn a percentage of every service ticket you close, every door you sell, and every door you install, figured on the whole ticket, with nothing deducted for parts. Sell a service plan and there is a flat bonus on top of that.

The full structure

Hourly base

$16.67 per hour, which is the Arizona minimum wage plus 10%. Every hour you work is paid at this rate and it moves up automatically each January when the state rate does. Commission is added on top of hourly; it never replaces it.

Service work

  • 8% of every service ticket, on the total ticket.Service fee, repairs, springs, motor-only sales. Nothing deducted for parts.
  • Your rate steps up with your weekly total, and the higher rate is paid on your whole week, back to the first dollar.Cross $5,000 and the whole week pays 9%. Cross $10,000 and the whole week pays 15%.
  • Door sales and door installs are a separate pay structure. They do not count toward the service steps, and service does not count toward door pay.

How it gets paid

  • Pay week runs Monday through Sunday, paid by direct deposit the following Thursday.
  • Service commission pays the week the job is completed and paid.
  • Door commissions, both the sale and the install, pay in the week the install is completed. Not at the sale, not at the deposit.
  • Commission is figured from FieldPulse. The job must be invoiced, paid and closed out to count. Financed jobs count at the full ticket.
  • Prices come from the price book. Any discount needs its reason line on the estimate.
  • You get one shot at the same-day close. After that the office works the quote, and you still earn the 5% base when it closes, as long as you are still employed here.
  • Sold jobs are banked. If you quit or are terminated, every job that was already sold before your last day still pays you when the work is done. A quote that closes after your last day belongs to the house.
  • One plan for everyone. When it changes, it changes for the whole team at once, with notice.

Things to understand about the position

Read every line. This is the boundary, set before you are hired. All of it goes in the employee handbook and every employee signs off on it.

  • This is the commission structure. There are no exceptions. We do not do straight commission.
  • The small hourly is there to cover the misc running around: parts runs, shop time, the stuff that is not a ticket.
  • The schedule is flexible, but once your schedule is set, you are expected to be available for it.
  • We provide Arizona mandated sick time (1 hour earned for every 30 hours worked, up to 24 hours per year) that can be used however you want. However, if you call in FIRST THING in the morning with an excuse as to why you cannot work your schedule too many times, you will no longer be employed here.
  • You are off on the MAJOR holidays: Christmas, New Years and Thanksgiving. Any other holiday off requires 5 days ADVANCE notice.
  • That applies to ANY day off you want. We promote a healthy work/life balance! Need a day off mid-week for golf? Vegas with the boys? Just need a long weekend to do nothing? That's fine, live your life. Just let us know in ADVANCE and we won't book jobs for you. Simple!
  • Lateness costs you. Your first job starts within the first 30 minutes of your block. Late = a 20% deduction of that week's commissions. Continual tardiness = termination.
  • Damage to customer or company property is deducted at 50% of the cost. Flagrant or intentional damage is deducted at 100%.
  • Side work without explicit permission = immediate termination. That is THEFT.
  • Any theft, including time theft and giving away product or labor without invoicing it, = immediate termination.
  • We are a small, growing business looking for TEAM PLAYERS, not divas. I don't give a fuck what your "average ticket at A1 was." Don't tell me, show me. We are all here to make money, but we operate as a team, we help each other out, we stay flexible in an environment that changes constantly, and we do what needs to be done to build 5 star customers. Focus on the LITTLE THINGS. Doesn't sound like a fit? Don't apply. You will not last long.
  • Warranty work will not be tolerated. We do not claw back pay, but if sloppy work becomes your standard, you will be terminated. Slow down, focus on the little things, get it right the first time. You are paid hourly, what's your rush?
  • Quick to Hire, Quick to Fire. Show up on time, do good work, support your team = long term stability. Show up late, call in sick often, do sloppy work = out the door. Simple.
  • Every vehicle has a GPS device with VIDEO and AUDIO that management can tap into at its discretion. Don't like it, don't apply. Tampering with the device = termination.
